FURSAD FAQ.

Clear answers about the brand and the product while it is still in development.

What is FURSAD?

FURSAD is a Nepali biscuit and snack brand being developed around everyday tea-time occasions.

What is FURSAD Crunchy Tea Cracker?

It is a lightly sweet, lightly salted, extra-crunchy biscuit designed specifically to pair with chiya.

Is FURSAD available in Nepal?

The product is currently in development and is planned for launch in Nepal.

What does FURSAD mean?

Fursad refers to free time or a small break, reflecting the brand's focus on everyday tea breaks.

Is FURSAD spicy?

No. The current product concept deliberately focuses on a familiar, balanced tea-biscuit taste rather than masala or strong spices.

How much will FURSAD cost?

The target launch price is Rs 20, subject to final commercial production specifications.

Why only one SKU?

Because the first priority is repeat purchase. FURSAD is focusing on getting one tea cracker right before expanding the range.

A Nepali biscuit brand being built around one simple everyday ritual: chiya and a little fursad.
