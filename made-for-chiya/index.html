MADE FOR CHIYA

A biscuit built
around a moment.

FURSAD begins with a simple belief: some of the strongest food brands are built around a repeatable occasion, not a long list of flavours.

Why chiya?

Chiya appears in ordinary moments across Nepal: morning routines, shop counters, offices, homes, visits, conversations and short breaks between work. FURSAD is being designed to fit naturally into that existing habit.

What “Made for Chiya” means

It is not just a tagline. It is a product brief. The cracker needs enough crunch to remain interesting beside tea, enough salt to feel savoury, enough sweetness to feel familiar, and a clean enough finish that the next sip still tastes like chiya.

Why keep the flavour simple?

Our first product is not meant to impress through an unusual spice. The goal is repeat purchase. Familiarity lowers the barrier to trial; texture and balance create the reason to return.

Why only one SKU?

Focus makes product development more honest. Instead of spreading attention across several flavours, FURSAD is beginning with one tea cracker and one question: would people choose it again with their next cup of chiya?

Chiya. Crunch. Fursad.

A Nepali biscuit brand being built around one simple everyday ritual: chiya and a little fursad.
